# Welcome to Policy to Action

Defensible, policy-grounded decision support for your district's administrators

District onboarding edition · Version 1.0

## Why Districts Use Policy to Action

Every administrative decision in a school district happens against a backdrop of board policy, and the administrators making those decisions rarely have time to search a three-hundred-page manual mid-incident. Policy to Action closes that gap. An administrator describes the situation in plain language and receives structured guidance built exclusively from your district's own adopted policies and handbooks, with every claim cited back to the exact source text.

The word that matters is **defensible**. Answers are not generic best practices from the open internet. They are your policies, applied to your situation, with the receipts attached.

## How It Works

1

**Your policies come in once.** A designated administrator imports your board policy manual directly from your policy website (BoardDocs and similar platforms are supported) or from a spreadsheet, and uploads your student and staff handbooks. Setup typically takes under twenty minutes.

2

**Administrators describe real scenarios.** A student altercation involving an IEP. A parent records request. A staff leave question. The platform understands multi-part situations and answers each embedded question.

3

**Answers arrive structured for action.** Each answer summarizes the policies that apply, lists numbered recommended actions, and spells out the implications, with citation chips linking every claim to source text.

## The Evidence Standard

Any answer is only as strong as its sources, so the platform makes verification a one-click habit. Selecting any citation opens the evidence panel with the exact policy language the answer was grounded in, and a path to the complete policy text.

![The evidence panel showing the quoted source text behind an answer](screenshots/05-evidence-drawer.jpg)

Figure 3. The evidence panel: the exact source text, one click from every claim.

**Institutional knowledge that compounds:** Administrators can pin answers to build a living FAQ for recurring situations, and every conversation is saved to searchable history. The platform gets more valuable the longer your team uses it.

## Your First Week

| Day         | Activity                                                                                                                             |
|-------------|--------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------|
| Day 1       | Your setup administrator creates the workspace and imports policies and handbooks using the District Setup Guide.                    |
| Day 2       | Each administrator creates an account and verifies email. Individual accounts keep each person's conversations and pins private.     |
| Days 3 to 5 | Each administrator asks two or three real scenarios from their current caseload and verifies the citations against the source panel. |
| End of week | Team huddle: compare answers, pin the guidance worth keeping, and identify any policy areas where the manual itself needs attention. |

## Privacy and Security Posture

- Your district's dataset is private to your workspace and never shared across districts.
- Accounts require verified email; passwords are stored with modern one-way hashing, never in plain text.
- Administrators are coached to use placeholders such as "Student A" rather than student names, and the interface reinforces this on every screen.
- Answers are generated from your uploaded sources; the platform is decision support, not legal advice, and every answer says so.
