# Domain Research Report: Policy to Action Platform

Prepared July 31, 2026 for Beau Scott, Scarlet Fire LLC
Scope: identify and validate at least 10 purchasable domains aligned with the Policy to Action platform and the supporting dissertation study

## Executive Summary

Fifty-plus registry lookups across three rounds produced 16 confirmed-available .com candidates, plus 6 available .app and 5 highly likely available .ai companions. The top recommendations are policyaligned.com, defensibleaction.com, and policybacked.com, each of which carries the full .com, .ai, and .app set unclaimed, an increasingly rare alignment of brand and inventory.

One strategic finding warrants immediate attention: policytoaction.com is already registered and currently displays a Squarespace page titled "Policy to Action" with an "under construction" notice. If you do not control that registration, a third party is incubating something under your shipping brand name. If you do control it, the incumbent-brand question answers itself. Either way, this materially informs the rebrand decision.

## Naming Inputs

The candidate slate was engineered from three source layers:

1. The platform itself: decision support for K-12 administrators that returns structured, citation-backed guidance built exclusively from a district's own board policies and handbooks. Brand language in production: "Answers with receipts," "Defensibility is the default," "Every answer, grounded in your district's own policies."
2. The dissertation (Augmented Decision-Making: The Roles of Just-in-Time Learning and Cognitive Load in AI-Supported K-12 Leadership): recurring constructs include policy-aligned guidance, action planning, policy navigation, procedural fidelity, defensibility, just-in-time performance support, and augmentation that preserves human judgment.
3. Operational reality: the iOS app is in App Store review as "Policy to Action" by Scarlet Fire LLC, and production runs at policytoaction.beauhawked.com.

## Method

- Authoritative source: registry RDAP endpoints (Verisign for .com, Google Registry for .app). A "not found" registry response means the name is unregistered and purchasable at standard pricing.
- Cross-verification: independent DNS resolution checks (NXDOMAIN confirmation) on all headline candidates.
- .ai lookups used DNS verification (the .ai registry does not expose a comparable public RDAP endpoint); confirm at registrar checkout.
- Trademark screen: web search for existing products or companies using the finalist names.
- Checks performed July 31, 2026 (US Eastern). Availability is a live market; unregistered names can be taken by anyone at any time.

## Estimated Carrying Costs

- .com: roughly 10 to 15 dollars per year at mainstream registrars
- .app: roughly 14 to 20 dollars per year (requires HTTPS, which Vercel provides by default)
- .ai: roughly 70 to 100 dollars per year, historically sold on multi-year initial terms
- Registering a winner across all three TLDs runs roughly 100 to 135 dollars per year, inexpensive insurance for a brand underpinning both a dissertation study and a commercial launch

## Risk Notes

1. policytoaction.com collision. The registered domain presents a Squarespace "under construction" page titled "Policy to Action." Please confirm whether this registration is yours. If it is not, treat it as a live brand-collision risk for the incumbent name and weigh the rebrand candidates accordingly.
2. actonpolicy.com adjacency. Act-On Software is an established marketing automation vendor. The markets differ, but the phonetic overlap justifies a trademark screen before committing.
3. Trademark clearance generally. No exact-match conflicts surfaced for PolicyAligned, DefensibleAction, or PolicyBacked in web screening, but this research is not legal advice; a formal USPTO and state-registry search by a trademark attorney is the prudent final gate for whichever name wins.
4. Registry premium tiers. Google (.app) and the .ai registry price some names at premium rates that registry lookups do not reveal. Confirm standard pricing at checkout; these compound names are unlikely to be premium-flagged.
5. iOS cutover timing. The Capacitor shell pins navigation to policytoaction.beauhawked.com. Moving production to a purchased domain requires an ios/ configuration change, an Xcode rebuild, and a resubmission. Sequence any domain cutover after the current App Store review concludes.
6. Market timing. Unregistered names are first come, first served. Once a finalist is chosen, register same-day.
