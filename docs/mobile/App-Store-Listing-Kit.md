## Description

Policy to Action is decision support for school district administrators. Describe a real scenario in plain language, such as a student conduct incident, a records request, or a staff leave question, and receive structured, citation-backed guidance built exclusively from your district's own board policies and handbooks.

Every answer names the policies that apply, lists numbered recommended actions, and spells out the implications, with a citation for every source used. The evidence panel quotes the exact policy language behind each claim, so you can verify guidance against the governing text before you act. Defensibility is the default.

YOUR SOURCES, YOUR CONTROL
A designated administrator imports the board policy manual and uploads student and staff handbooks once; most districts finish setup in under twenty minutes. Everything lives in a searchable library your district owns, and answers are grounded only in what your district has uploaded.

BUILT FOR REAL ADMINISTRATIVE WORK
Multi-part situations are handled issue by issue and tailored to your role. Pin answers worth keeping, revisit full conversation history, read source documents in the built-in reader, and print clean copies for board packets. Your workspace stays in sync across the app and the web platform.

PRIVATE BY DESIGN
No advertising, no trackers, no selling of data. Content is processed under a signed data processing agreement and is never used to train AI models. Export or delete your data at any time from Settings.

Policy to Action is a product of Scarlet Fire LLC. Guidance is decision support, not legal advice; verify consequential decisions against the cited source text.
