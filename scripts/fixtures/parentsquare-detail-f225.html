F225

FUNDRAISING & CROWDFUNDING

Structural fixture paragraph one: fundraising activities conducted in the name of the Corporation or any of its schools must be approved in advance by the building principal.

Structural fixture paragraph two: crowdfunding campaigns are subject to the same review and all funds raised are receipted into the appropriate extracurricular account.

Adopted: 2.14.24

Revised: 12.10.25
