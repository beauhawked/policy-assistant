A parent requested their child's education records. How quickly must we respond?
Under policy JRA and FERPA, the district must provide access within 45 days of the request. Your student handbook goes further, committing to 10 school days for routine records requests — that shorter timeline is the one families will hold you to.
Recommended actions 1. Log the request date today — the clock started when it was received. 2. Verify the requester's custodial rights before release (JRA §2). 3. Schedule delivery within 10 school days per the handbook commitment.

JRA Student records
Section J — Students·Adopted Sep 2011·Revised Mar 2025·Board policies 2026 CopyAsk about this policy
The district recognizes the right of parents and eligible students to inspect and review the student's education records. Parents may inspect and review the student's education records within forty-five (45) days of the district receiving a request for access. 2. Verification of identity. Before releasing records, the custodian of records shall verify the identity and custodial rights of the requester. In cases of divorce or separation, both parents retain access rights unless a court order on file provides otherwise. 3. Fees. The district may charge a reasonable fee for copies of records unless the fee effectively prevents the parent or eligible student from exercising their right to inspect and review. 4. Amendment requests. Parents who believe information in the education records is inaccurate or misleading may request amendment through the procedure set out in regulation JRA-R.
